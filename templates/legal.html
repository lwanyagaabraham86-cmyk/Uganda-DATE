Community Guidelines

Uganda Dating should feel welcoming, respectful and safe for adults. Treat people as people, not as products.

Do

Child Safety Standards

Uganda Dating is an adults-only service. People under 18 are not permitted to create or use dating accounts.

Zero tolerance

Any content or behaviour that sexualizes, exploits or endangers children is prohibited and may be removed immediately and reported to the appropriate authorities when required.

Report concerns

Use the in-app Report feature to flag a child-safety concern. Our moderation process is intended to review reports promptly.

Child-safety point of contact

For urgent child-safety concerns, use the in-app Report feature and contact the Uganda Dating moderation team at admin@ugandadating.app.
